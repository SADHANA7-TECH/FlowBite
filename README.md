# FlowBite

## Intelligent Restaurant Operations Platform

FlowBite is a full-stack SaaS platform designed to streamline restaurant operations by connecting customers, kitchen staff, waitstaff, and managers through one unified real-time system.

## User Stories Completed

- Customers can browse the digital menu and place orders.
- Customers can join a virtual waitlist.
- Kitchen staff can view and manage incoming orders through a Kitchen Display System.
- Waitstaff can manage tables and customer queues.
- Managers can monitor restaurant analytics and operations.
- Managers can track inventory and low-stock ingredients.
- Managers can manage menu item availability.
- AI-powered insights provide operational recommendations.
- Real-time updates synchronize restaurant operations across different roles.
- Restaurant operations are managed through a unified dashboard.

## AI Usage

Google Gemini API is used as an AI-powered operational assistant.

The AI analyzes restaurant data such as:

- Sales trends
- Order activity
- Inventory levels
- Popular menu items
- Operational bottlenecks

Based on this data, the AI generates insights and recommendations to help managers improve:

- Demand forecasting
- Inventory management
- Staffing decisions
- Food waste reduction
- Overall operational efficiency

## How It Works

FlowBite connects:

Customer → Kitchen → Waitstaff → Manager

When a customer places an order, the order is immediately sent to the kitchen. Kitchen staff can update the order status, while waitstaff can manage tables and queues. Managers can monitor real-time analytics, inventory, and AI-generated operational insights from a centralized dashboard.
